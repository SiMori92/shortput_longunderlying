## Explicit interpretations and limits

- Premium denominator is **RV20**; the brief does not choose an RV window. Missing same-day manual observations break the 20-session streak; nothing is carried forward.
- Near-delta means **|delta| = .20 ± .05**, configurable. Zero-mid, expired and too-wide quotes fail. Inclusive boundaries use a tiny rounding tolerance; confirmed no-listed-options evidence forces the options check to fail.
- All configured unlocks require sourced actual dates and 20 complete subsequent sessions to pass. Approximate schedule/fractions originate in the brief. Scheduled-date statistics are labelled scenarios. No return/volatility pass threshold was invented.
- Readiness also requires all seven fact fields, final validated closes, identity and history. This is conservative research qualification, not a trading suggestion.
- Tracking compares daily raw-price returns, compounds daily leveraged reference returns and resets to the last contiguous overlap after missing sessions. It is not total-return tracking and does not infer distributions.
- The known calendar ends in 2028. Unlock dates beyond the reviewed calendar fail schema validation. The history-date projection begins at the last stored close, not the current date, so stale history cannot silently shift the estimate.
- Existing React state/Zod/CSS/Recharts were sufficient; no new dependencies. M8 still owns login issuance and full page/read-route auth. Existing session checks protect all new manual writes; loopback binding remains in place.
- Unreviewed **SOXX 3:1 split on 5 Nov 2026**, the two missing **^IRX** sessions, and absent real account state still block core recommendations. No data-review exception or strategy change was introduced.

No open implementation question blocks M6. IV window/delta tolerance and corporate-action confirmations are recorded policy/evidence choices; they do not silently claim market eligibility. M7 may proceed only on the user's next milestone instruction.
